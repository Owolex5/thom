About Me

I'm Thomas Liam McCarthy, a lobbyist and security analyst from Galway, Ireland. My work has two sides that depend on each other: persuading decision makers to support policies that help my government overseas, and understanding who might want to harm the country.

On the lobbying side, I push for policies and votes that influence my government's interests abroad and keep the relationship between both countries healthy. On the security side, I analyse possible threats and targets, and I look closely at high value targets and people of interest who may be connected to an act of terrorism, foreign or domestic.
